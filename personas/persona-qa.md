# Persona Q&A

For each role: what they care about, the question they'll ask, how to answer, and the trap.

## CEO

- **Cares about:** the commitments made to the board; speed to visible results.
- **Asks:** "How fast does this show up in the numbers?"
- **Answer shape:** Tie to *their* stated commitment. Offer the proof timeline, not a promise: "Six weeks to a measured result on a defined scope."
- **Trap:** Selling a transformation. Sell a proof.

## CRO

- **Cares about:** relationships, promo spend, not burdening reps.
- **Asks:** "Does this add work for my team?"
- **Answer shape:** Show the approval step as one action, not a new workflow. Show where the recommendation *replaces* a manual step.
- **Trap:** Implying the system overrides rep judgment. It informs it, and a person approves.

## VP RevOps

- **Cares about:** data quality, integrations, the weekly cadence.
- **Asks:** "What data do you need, and what breaks when it's wrong?"
- **Answer shape:** Be specific about inputs. Show the data-quality gate and what happens when it trips.
- **Trap:** Hand-waving data prep. This is your champion; be honest about the work.

## CTO

- **Cares about:** security, integration effort, vendor risk.
- **Asks:** "Where does our data go, and how does this connect?"
- **Answer shape:** Answer directly or say "I'll get you the written answer by [date]." Never guess on security or certifications. Check any compliance claim with the right internal owner before stating it.
- **Trap:** Overclaiming certifications. One wrong claim ends the deal.

## CFO

- **Cares about:** return, and how anyone would know.
- **Asks:** "What does it return, and how do we know?"
- **Answer shape:** "The sizing is a modeled range with no close rate applied. The proof sets a conservative realization rate, a control group of comparable doors, and a go/no-go on incremental cases." Show `proof/sizing.py` output.
- **Trap:** Presenting the modeled figure as a forecast. Always label it sizing.

## Chief AI Officer

- **Cares about:** trust, governance, auditability.
- **Asks:** "How do we trust it, and how do we govern it?"
- **Answer shape:** Every recommendation shows its reasons, math, and confidence. A person approves before release. A drift gate watches data quality. Outcomes are written back so results are measured, not assumed.
- **Trap:** Saying "the model is accurate." Show the controls instead.
